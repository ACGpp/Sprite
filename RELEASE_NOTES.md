Download **arm64.dmg** for Apple Silicon or **x64.dmg** for Intel (macOS 14+). Open it, drag Sprite to Applications, then launch and configure your model provider. Node, pi and the kernel are included; no developer tools are required. ZIP alternatives and SHA-256 checksums are also attached.

Current builds are ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. If macOS blocks the app, verify its source and allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Back up the entire `~/.claude-memory/` before upgrading. Quit Sprite before replacing the app. There is no automatic updater.
